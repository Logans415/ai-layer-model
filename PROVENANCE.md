# Authorship and provenance

The **AI Layer Model** was developed by **Logan Smith**, who maintains this repository as the canonical source for its definitions, documentation and mapping skill.

[Canonical repository](https://github.com/Logans415/ai-layer-model) · [Citation metadata](CITATION.cff) · [Change history](CHANGELOG.md)

## Origin and development

The model grew from a practical integration problem: understanding the responsibilities of AI tools and how they fit into an existing technology stack. An initial six-layer sketch developed into nine runtime layers grouped into Substrate, Cognition and Systems, with governance and other responsibilities spanning the stack.

Version **0.4.0** introduces the public reference, worked examples, structured layer definitions and a portable mapping skill. This initial release focuses on communicating the model and inviting evidence-based discussion of its boundaries and applications.

## Version history

Tagged releases identify published versions. [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) records substantive revisions, and the Git history records changes to the repository. Release publication dates establish the public distribution history; earlier private development records are maintained separately.

## Relationship to other work

The model uses an OSI-inspired separation of concerns to organize AI responsibilities. [Related work](docs/related-work.md) identifies complementary architecture and threat-modeling approaches. It is an independent reference model, open to refinement through practical use and discussion.
